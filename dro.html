Disaster Awareness & Water Safety Guide

What is Drought?

A drought is a natural disaster where there is a long period of very low rainfall and shortage of water. It affects agriculture, animals, humans, and the environment. Drought can create serious problems for food and water supply.

Warning Signs

  • Very low rainfall for a long time
  • Dry rivers, lakes, and water sources
  • Cracks appearing in dry soil
  • Crop damage and poor farming conditions
  • Shortage of drinking water

Prevention & Safety Measures

  • Save water and avoid unnecessary usage.
  • Store clean water during warnings.
  • Use water carefully for farming.
  • Plant trees and protect natural resources.
  • Follow government water guidelines.
  • Prepare emergency water supplies.

Emergency Details

🚨 Emergency Number: 112

🚑 Ambulance: 108

👮 Police: 100

🌐 Disaster Helpline: 1078

High Risk Areas

  • Desert regions
  • Low rainfall areas
  • Agricultural zones
  • Dry climate regions
  • Water shortage areas
