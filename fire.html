Disaster Awareness & Fire Safety Guide

What is Fire Disaster?

A fire disaster is a dangerous situation where uncontrolled fire causes damage to people, property, and the environment. It can occur due to electrical faults, gas leakage, accidents, or unsafe activities.

Warning Signs

  • Smoke or burning smell
  • Sparks from electrical equipment
  • Gas leakage smell
  • Overheated wires or devices
  • Sudden increase in temperature

Prevention & Safety Measures

  • Keep flammable items away from fire sources.
  • Check electrical wiring regularly.
  • Do not ignore gas leakage signs.
  • Keep fire extinguishers ready.
  • Leave the building immediately during fire.
  • Do not use elevators during fire emergencies.

Emergency Details

🚨 Emergency Number: 112

🚒 Fire Service: 101

🚑 Ambulance: 108

👮 Police: 100

High Risk Areas

  • Factories
  • Electrical rooms
  • Gas storage areas
  • Crowded buildings
  • Forest areas
