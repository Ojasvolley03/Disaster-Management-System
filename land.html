🌍 What is a Landslide?

A landslide is the sudden movement of rocks, soil and debris down a slope. It can happen due to heavy rainfall, earthquakes, weak soil or human activities.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • Cracks in roads and walls
  • Falling rocks
  • Leaning trees or poles
  • Sudden muddy water flow
  • Unusual ground movement

🛡️ Precautions

  • Avoid steep slopes
  • Do not build near mountains
  • Keep drainage clean
  • Plant trees to prevent soil erosion
  • Follow weather updates

🚨 Instant Steps

  • Stay calm
  • Move away from slope areas
  • Go to open safe locations
  • Protect head and body
  • Follow rescue instructions

🏠 After Landslide Safety

  • Check injuries and give first aid
  • Do not enter damaged buildings
  • Avoid blocked roads
  • Watch for another landslide
  • Report danger zones
