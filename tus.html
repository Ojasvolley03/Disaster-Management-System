🌍 What is Tsunami?

A tsunami is a series of powerful ocean waves caused by underwater earthquakes, volcanic eruptions or landslides. It can cause severe flooding and destruction in coastal areas.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • 🌊 Sudden sea level rise
  • 🌍 Strong earthquake near coast
  • 🐚 Ocean water moving backward
  • 🔊 Unusual ocean sounds
  • 📢 Tsunami warning alerts

🛡️ Precautions

  • Move to higher ground
  • Know evacuation routes
  • Keep emergency kit ready
  • Avoid coastal areas
  • Follow official updates

🚨 Instant Steps

  • Act quickly
  • Leave beach immediately
  • Move away from water
  • Do not wait for warnings
  • Help children and elderly

🏠 After Tsunami Safety

  • Check injuries and give first aid
  • Avoid flood water
  • Do not enter damaged buildings
  • Watch for more waves
  • Report damaged areas
