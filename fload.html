Disaster Awareness & Safety Guide

What is Flood?

A flood is a natural disaster where water covers normally dry land. It can happen due to heavy rainfall, river overflow, dam failure, or poor drainage systems.

Warning Signs / Symptoms

  • Continuous heavy rainfall
  • Water level rising in rivers
  • Dark clouds and severe storms
  • Water entering streets and homes
  • Government flood warnings

Prevention & Safety Measures

  • Move to higher ground during warnings
  • Keep emergency supplies ready
  • Do not travel through moving water
  • Protect important documents
  • Follow disaster management instructions

Emergency Details

🚨 Emergency Number: 112
🚑 Ambulance: 108
🚒 Fire Service: 101
👮 Police: 100

High Risk Areas

  • River side locations
  • Low lying areas
  • Coastal regions
  • Areas with poor drainage
