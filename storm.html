🌪️ What is a Storm?

A storm is a powerful weather event with strong winds, heavy rainfall, lightning and sometimes hail. It can damage buildings, trees, roads and electricity systems.

⚠️ Warning Signs

  • 🌩️ Dark heavy clouds
  • 💨 Sudden strong winds
  • 🌧️ Heavy rainfall
  • ⚡ Lightning and thunder
  • 📢 Weather alerts

🛡️ Precautions

  • Stay indoors during storms
  • Secure doors and windows
  • Keep emergency kit ready
  • Charge phones and power banks
  • Avoid open fields

🚨 Instant Steps

  • Stay calm
  • Move away from windows
  • Switch off electricity if needed
  • Avoid touching wires
  • Follow emergency instructions

🏠 After Storm Safety

  • Check injuries and give first aid
  • Avoid damaged buildings
  • Stay away from fallen wires
  • Clean dangerous areas carefully
  • Report damage to authorities
