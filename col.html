🌊 What is a Cyclone?

A cyclone is a powerful rotating storm formed over warm ocean water. It brings extremely strong winds, heavy rainfall, flooding and destruction. Cyclones can damage houses, trees, roads and electricity systems.

⚠️ Warning Signs of Cyclone

  • 🌧️ Continuous heavy rainfall
  • 🌬️ Strong and increasing winds
  • 🌊 Sea water level rising
  • ☁️ Dark rotating clouds
  • 📢 Government cyclone warnings
  • ⚡ Frequent lightning and storms

🛡️ Cyclone Precautions Before Storm

  • ✔️ Keep emergency food and drinking water
  • ✔️ Charge mobile phones and power banks
  • ✔️ Keep important documents safe
  • ✔️ Move away from coastal areas if evacuation is announced
  • ✔️ Close doors and windows properly
  • ✔️ Remove loose objects from roofs
  • ✔️ Keep a first-aid kit ready
  • ✔️ Listen to official weather updates

🚨 During Cyclone - Instant Steps

  • ⚠️ Stay inside a strong building
  • Move away from windows and glass areas
  • Do not go outside to watch the storm
  • Switch off electricity if flooding starts
  • Stay calm and help children and elderly people
  • Follow emergency instructions

🌊 After Cyclone Safety

  • Check for injuries and provide first aid
  • Do not touch damaged electrical wires
  • Avoid flood water because it may contain bacteria
  • Report damaged roads or buildings
  • Return home only after official permission
